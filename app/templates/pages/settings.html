Privacy & data

What is stored

Your username, optional email, a salted password hash (never the password itself), and your work: {{ project_count }} project{{ 's' if project_count != 1 }} with tasks, requirement checks, prompts, papers and report drafts. Nothing is shared with other users, and nothing is sold.

What is sent to the AI

When you use a tool, the project's name, deadline, type, description and guideline plus your answers are sent to Google's Gemini API. Emails and phone numbers are masked first, but other personal details are not, so don't enter addresses or ID numbers. Depending on Google's terms for the plan in use, inputs may be used to improve its models.

What is sent to the paper database

Only short English search terms go to OpenAlex, never your description or guideline.

Cookies and local storage

One session cookie keeps you logged in. Your theme and sidebar choices are saved in this browser. There is no tracking or advertising.

Download my data
Everything above as one JSON file: your profile (without the password hash) and all projects.

Delete account

This permanently deletes your account and every project, task, prompt, paper and report draft. It can't be undone. Download your data first if you want a copy.
